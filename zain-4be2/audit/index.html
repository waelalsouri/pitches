Before we run a single ad

Seven fixes I found

Small changes, in priority order, so the traffic the webinar brings in doesn't leak.

1
Do first

YouTube says productivity, the funnel says sales

Bio, banner, avatar and links all tell a different story from the free training. A new viewer can't tell what you do in three seconds.

Bio · now"Helping Muslims become productive without sacrificing their deen."
Bio · change to"I help Muslims earn $2k to $15k a month remotely, closing calls for halal businesses. Free training ↓"
Banner · now"MAXIMISE PRODUCTIVTY & DEEN" (typo, off-niche)
Banner · change to"Halal income. Remote sales. Free live training every month." + your face
Avatar · nowBoxing headgear, face hidden
Avatar · change toThe clean studio headshot used on the register page
Description links · now2 to 3 links per video
Description links · change toOne link: the webinar registration page
2
Do first

Upload every reel to YouTube Shorts

Shorts are free reach on the platform where you already have 14.8K subs. Same file, zero extra work.

3
This week

Instagram profile

Your IG is the first place a webinar registrant checks you out.

Name field · now"Zain Bilal"
Name field · change to"Zain | Halal Remote Sales" (the name field is searchable)
Highlights · nowIncludes a free-course highlight
Highlights · change toRemove it. Add: Start here · Student wins · Is it halal? · Free training
Pinned posts · nowNot structured
Pinned posts · change to3 pins: your story (uni dropout to closer), a student win, the webinar invite
4
Do first

Setters on every comment and DM

Every commenter and DMer is a lead. Without a setter replying inside an hour, most go cold. Two part-time setters on commission cover it.

5
This week

Let the webinar qualify, not the free course

The free course works as a magnet, but it attracts people you then have to qualify by hand. The webinar does the teaching, and the Typeform under the replay asks the qualifying questions (situation, hours, income goal, ability to invest).

6
This week

Make Noble Circle findable

Nobody can join what they can't find. Put it on Skool (or similar), link it from the replay page and name it in every email.

7
Next 30 days

Borrow audiences: podcasts and hijrah creators

Pitch Muslim podcasts and creators talking about hijrah, halal income and leaving the 9 to 5. One appearance a week, each one sending people to the webinar.
