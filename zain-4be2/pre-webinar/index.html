Hey {FIRST NAME},

Quick one, because a lot of people sign up without knowing what a closer is.

A business (a coach, an agency, an education company) gets people to book a call. Someone has to take that call, find out if the person's a fit, and help them decide. That someone is the closer.

You don't build the product, run ads or post content. You take calls from people who already asked to speak to someone.

You get paid a percentage of every sale you close. That's the whole model, and on Sunday I'll show you how to get your first role.
