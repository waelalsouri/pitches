When I started, I'd build a site for anyone who'd pay. A barber one week, a gym the next. Every project started from zero.

The day I picked one niche, my prices went up and my hours went down. Same skills. Different focus.

On your call, my team will help you pick yours. Come with three names you already know in business. [CONFIRM: swap in a student story]
